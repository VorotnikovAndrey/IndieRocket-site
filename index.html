Marketing toolkit for indie games on Steam

IndieRocket is a desktop app for Windows that helps small studios and solo developers grow wishlists: understand Steam analytics, polish the store page, cut short videos from gameplay and publish them.

Steam analyticsWishlists, traffic and clear next steps from your Steamworks reports.
Store page auditA checklist and suggestions for capsules, descriptions and tags.
Video factoryTurn gameplay recordings into vertical clips with captions, music and an end card.
PublishingUpload finished clips to YouTube Shorts and to your TikTok drafts in a few clicks.

How publishing to TikTok works

  • You connect your own TikTok account with the official “Login with TikTok” flow in your browser.
  • You pick a finished clip in IndieRocket and send it to TikTok. By default the video goes to your TikTok drafts (inbox), where you add music, a caption and post it yourself.
  • IndieRocket never posts anything without your explicit action and confirmation.
  • Everything runs on your computer. IndieRocket has no servers and does not collect your data — see the Privacy Policy.
